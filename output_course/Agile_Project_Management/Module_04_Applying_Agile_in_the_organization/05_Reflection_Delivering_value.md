# 📝 Reflection: Delivering value

---

### Câu 1:

In the Penta case study reading, you learned how the company benefits from applying the Scrum framework. As a thought experiment, let’s work through how an Agile approach can help project teams increase value delivery. Remember that Agile teams deliver value by building the right thing, building the thing right, and running it right.

Now that you know more about value-driven delivery, you can put that knowledge into practice.

Are you ready?

---

### Câu 2:

Think of a product or service you use regularly. It can be a real or imaginary device (e.g. smartphone, universal remote) or software (e.g. email, social networking, video conferencing). Consider two or three of its most important features: How do these features create value for different customers? If your team were to build this product or service, what would you do to make sure the team builds the right thing? Write 3-4 sentences summarizing your response.

---

### Câu 3:

Your job as a project manager goes beyond the product development process. You need to continue delivering value to customers once your product or service is out in the world. Write 3-4 sentences about what you can do to make sure your team runs it right.
