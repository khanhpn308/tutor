!Forming the Past Perfect Progressive. Fill in the missing words with the past perfect progressive.  
Use the verb in the parentheses ( ) after the blank.  
1\. By 2:00 yesterday, John _____________________ (work) on his homework for ten hours.  
2\. When the earthquake happened, I _____________________ (sleep) for over an hour.  
3\. Joe _____________________ (drive) for three hours when his car ran out of gas.  
4. By last summer, Maria ______________________ (build) her new house for 3 years.
