Phrasal Modals and Modals Overview Practice  
Directions: Add to when it is necessary. Write x when to is not necessary.  
1. Sherry is able _____ run a mile in under 4 minutes.  
2. Would he _____ give me a ride home?  
3. You had better _____ leave earlier because there is a lot of traffic at this time.  
4. Annie can _____ bake very well, so everyone asks her to make something every week.  
5. He has got _____ talk to his girlfriend about the problem.  
6. We ought _____ watch the video again. I didn’t see that funny part.  
7. Should Jake _____ get a new pair of glasses? Her old glasses have a lot of scratches.  
8. You may _____ go to the restroom, but hurry up!  
9. The babies have _____ sleep now. It’s their nap time.  
10. Tomorrow, I will _____ fly home for the party.  
Directions: Fill in the correct form of the modal/phrasal modal.  
Example: The computer has to scan (have to scan) the file for viruses.  
1\. You _______________ (have to cut) the grass. It is getting too long!  
2\. You _______________ (must wear) your seatbelt when you drive.  
3. Jane isn’t going to the party because she _______________ (have got to take) care of her sick son.  
4. We _______________ (ought to buy) a lottery ticket. The prize is $150 million!  
5\. The teachers _______________ (should discuss) the classroom rules with the students at the beginning of  
the school term.  
6. He has many talents. He _______________ (be able to play) many sports, draw, cook, sing, fly an airplane,  
and make online games!  
7\. The party _______________ (have got to finish) by 10pm because we have to rush to the airport after.  
8\. Smartphones _______________ (be able to do) anything these days. How did people live without them  
before?  
9\. When winter comes in Chicago, you _______________ (have to wear) a long, thick jackets. It’s freezing!  
10\. You _______________ (ought to say) thank you when someone does something nice for you.
