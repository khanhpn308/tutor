### Câu 1:

Write an original dialogue between two people. Use at least three different modal verbs, and include at least one question and one statement using modals.

You may use modals such as:

can / can’t

could / couldn’t

may / might

would rather

had better

be able to

will / won’t

Example

Linda: Hi Paul. Can you help me with something?

Paul: Sure, I can, but I won't be able to help you until after school. 

Linda: That's ok. I would rather do this later than now anyway. 

Paul: What do you need help with? I might be able to guess. 

Linda: You may be able to guess, but I'd better explain. 

Paul: Ok. Explain. 

Linda: I am having trouble in my French class. I can't seem to understand past perfect tense. Could you help me review before my test?

Please use the example only as a model. Write your own original dialogue.

Formatting tip: Please write your dialogue in this format:

Character A: [Sentence] 

Character B: [Sentence]

Do you understand?.
