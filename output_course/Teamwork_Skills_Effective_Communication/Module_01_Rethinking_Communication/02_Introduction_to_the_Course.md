​Why is group communication so important? ​Well, for one, so much of our lives, both professional and personal, are mediated through small groups of other people. ​Our occupation and education, our recreation and socialization, our civic participation, and even religious expression. ​Some are more formal, some are informal, but almost all involve coordinating with small groups of other people ​to accomplish and experience whatever it is we desire in that situation. ​Of course, groups aren't always the answer, and sometimes should even be avoided. ​But so many areas of our lives involve or even require group work, ​and these groups almost always arise in response to some necessity or opportunity. ​There's something that needs to get done, a decision needs to be made, a problem needs to be solved, ​that requires the involvement of several people.
